What's New in v1.5.5

New Features:
Avalon Maps: Roads of Avalon maps are now fully integrated and visible on the radar.
Compass Directions: Added compass directions to the overlay interface for better navigation (#26).

Bug Fixes & Refinements:
Mob Classification: Fixed a parsing error where the "Chestnut Spirit" mob was incorrectly identified and displayed as a "Chest".
Mist Cages: Resolved detection and display issues related to Mist Cages.
Tracker UI: Fixed a rendering bug that caused Trackers to mistakenly draw a Crown icon on the overlay. 

Work in Progress (To-Do)
Packet-Based Mob Death Tracking: Currently developing a system to pinpoint a mob's exact death location based on incoming packet data. The goal is to successfully track mob health and death events even when player packets are out of range.(#23)
