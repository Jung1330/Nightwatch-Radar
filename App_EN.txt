What's New in v1.5.5

+ Roads of Avalon maps are now fully integrated and visible on the radar.
+ Compass directions added to the overlay interface (#26).
+ 106 new maps extracted from the game client: islands, arenas, Crystal Realm, Conqueror's Hall.
+ 51 maps upgraded to higher resolution (1024x1024).
+ The Mists dungeon (Knightfall Abbey) now renders with its real map.
~ The "Chestnut Spirit" mob was incorrectly identified as a "Chest" - fixed.
~ Detection and display issues with Mist Cages have been resolved.
~ Fixed a rendering bug where Trackers mistakenly drew a Crown icon.
